# Description

 [opus 5.5] #208 persistent-cache, milestone M5 cache-fill. The blocking findings still open after the last M5 review round (three review lenses at be7ba6bc, before the rebase onto #287 bulk-reads; the code they touch did not change in the rebase), with the probes that show each one. **M9 cache-serve must not answer any read from the message cache until this issue is closed.** Until then the cache only fills and measures (the shadow counters), and the README's operator section says to leave it off. 1. **R6-A, false completeness: erase_scope against a racing listing.** MessageCache::erase_scope bumps the channel epoch when it is called, but the writer applies Op::EraseScope later. A Main read whose stamp is taken after the bump, and whose observed_ms is no later than the writer's apply time (a backlogged queue, or the same millisecond), lands after the erase. merge::revives needs observed_ms > deleted_ms, so the tombstone holds the reply (a Slack reply also sent to the channel) back, yet Writer::listing still stamps Main's claim over its instant. Below the band that undoes coverage::puncture; being the latest read, it also writes checked_from_ms back down, which in the band undoes give_up_instant. A window of 4 three seconds later and the Main timeline fallback an hour later decide Serve without a message upstream still lists. On a bridge the same follows with no race, because Fill::observed takes min(as_of, start). A thread listing stamped in the same window can rebuild the erased thread's head and coverage over its tombstoned replies, since confirmed() unblocks the thread scope straight after the erase. Probes: `r6_a_a_main_read_begun_before_a_thread_erase_was_applied_never_reclaims_the_reply` (three variants) and `r6_b_a_page_across_the_reclaimed_instant_is_never_served` in /tmp/r6fc/head/vibe-talk/src/cache/r6_probes.rs, log /tmp/r6fc/r6a-head.log; `rv1_a_main_read_begun_while_the_erase_was_queued_never_reclaims_r1s_instant` and `rv1b_strictly_between_the_erase_call_and_its_apply` in /tmp/rvcc/probe-be7ba6bc/vibe-talk/src/cache/rv_probes.rs. Suggested fix, a weakening: in Writer::listing, take out of the claim (coverage::puncture) every instant whose listed row a tombstone newer than the listing's start held back, and raise checked_from_ms past it in the band; or give an erase's tombstones a time that no read stamped after the bump can fail to beat. Add the pause_writer test. 2. **R6-C, a deleted or edited message comes back: the channel epoch bump discards pushed deletions as Stale and records no drop.** erase_scope bumps the whole channel's epoch while keeping its other scopes, so observe_op answers Outcome::Stale for every queued observation of the channel stamped before the bump, pushed deletions and edits of messages the erase keeps included. Only ops_stale is counted: no mark_suspect, no lost(), last_drop_ms stays None, and removes_only() observations are not applied. fed_since then vouches for the old row, message_by_id decides Serve on a deleted message or on text edited away, and the store's summary of the old text is never orphaned. A read-scope request can set it off: a bogus thread id answered `thread_not_found` (or a Discord-protocol 404), confirmed by the server's own read ten minutes later, runs erase_scope; on a bridge, a deleted thread's 404 is confirmed while the adapter is pushing that thread's deletions. Probes: `r6_c_a_thread_erase_never_discards_a_queued_deletion_of_another_scopes_message` in /tmp/r6fc/head/vibe-talk/src/cache/r6_probes.rs, log /tmp/r6fc/r6c-head.log; `r6_a_bogus_thread_erase_never_lets_the_feed_vouch_for_a_deleted_message` and `r6_a_bogus_thread_erase_never_lets_the_feed_vouch_for_an_edited_messages_old_text` (control: `r6_control_a_pushed_deletion_lands`) in /tmp/r6priv/head/vibe-talk/src/cache/r6_probes.rs, logs /tmp/r6priv/r6-head.log and /tmp/r6priv/r6-prev.log. Suggested fix: on a Stale op for a channel whose rows survive the erase, apply its removes_only() observations and record a drop for everything else; or give scope erases an epoch of their own, once R6-A's weakening is in. 3. **AccessWatch block/unblock race**, named by two lenses as to be fixed before anything is served. report() and confirm() call listener.block and listener.unblock after the doubts lock is released, so a refusal that raises a new doubt while a success clears the old one can leave the channel in doubt but unblocked for up to CONFIRM_AFTER (10 minutes). Probe: `cache::access::r5_probe::r5_a_new_doubt_raised_while_an_old_one_clears_stays_blocked` in /tmp/r5cf/probe-head/vibe-talk/src/cache/access.rs. Fix: block and unblock while holding the doubts lock, or re-check stands() before unblocking. 4. **R5-A, a false miss that skews the numbers M9 is to be judged by.** fresh() judges a head by head_listed_ms, which advance_head pins to the read that opened the band for as long as it is open, so in a quiet channel a window, a count or an open page asked two seconds after a read of the newest end is Miss(StaleHead), for up to ten minutes after each new message. It never claims completeness, but it under-counts the shadow hit rate where the cache would help most. Probe: `r5_a_a_window_two_seconds_after_a_read_of_the_newest_end_is_not_stale` in /tmp/r5fc/probe/vibe-talk/src/cache/r5_probes.rs. Fix: measure freshness against checked_ms. Also open, non-blocking, to settle before M9: a control erase that fails (disk full, busy) is never retried and its block lives in memory only; erase_channels, erase_scope and erase_provider re-insert their pending entry with HashMap::insert, so an earlier erase can lower a later one's sequence, or re-insert u64::MAX after erase_file cleared the map; LiveHub::publish_read takes a fresh stamp at publish time rather than the tick's; a Discord-protocol provider's 403 or 404 counts as lost access whatever its body (an HTML 404 from a proxy would erase every channel); an AccessWatch provider key never given a dialect defaults to the more erasing DiscordProtocol; two reads of the newest end begun in the same millisecond can leave a deleted band row vouched for; give_up_instant rewrites a scope's coverage once per erased row.
