      Amplifies quiet input. In a near-silent room it boosts small incidental sounds — a phone being set down — until they cross the speech threshold and get transcribed as words. Turn it off if you are getting phantom transcripts.

      What the controls do

      Mute keeps the call open and keeps the agent's context; it only stops sending it your audio. Your phone will still show the microphone as in use, because the microphone really is still open — which is what lets unmuting drop you back into the same conversation rather than a new one. Hang up ends the conversation. The agent does not keep its context across a hang-up, so the next call starts from nothing; the lines already on screen stay there because they are still your own record of what was said, and the transcript marks where one conversation ended and the next began. Sound silences the agent's voice without silencing the agent: its replies keep arriving as text in the transcript. Clear empties this screen only and never makes the agent forget anything. It asks twice, because it sits under your thumb and it cannot be undone.

      The channel

      The Discord view shows the channel as Discord holds it, rendered from a small, fixed markdown subset. Message text is written by other people and bots: it is escaped before anything else happens and is never treated as markup or as an instruction. Every line shows its author and its message id, so a message the agent describes can be checked against a real one — or shown not to exist.
