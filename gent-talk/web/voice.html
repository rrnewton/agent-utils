      What the controls do

      Mute keeps the call open and keeps the agent's context; it only stops sending it your audio. Your phone will still show the microphone as in use, because the microphone really is still open — which is what lets unmuting drop you back into the same conversation rather than a new one. Hang up ends the conversation. The agent does not keep its context across a hang-up, so the next call starts from nothing; the lines already on screen stay there, and the transcript marks where one conversation ended and the next began. Clear view empties this screen only and never makes the agent forget anything.
